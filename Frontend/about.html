Our Mission

Making Legal Knowledge Accessible

Legal Aid Provider bridges the gap between ordinary citizens and the Indian legal system — giving everyone access to clear, reliable legal information for free.

The problem we solve

Legal Help Should Not Be a Luxury

In India, millions face legal disputes every day — with landlords, employers, companies, and government offices — but lack affordable legal guidance.

Most people don't know their rights under the Consumer Protection Act, cannot navigate the RTI process, or don't know how to respond when a landlord unlawfully withholds a deposit.

Legal Aid Provider uses AI to explain Indian law in plain, simple language — so any citizen can understand their rights and approach the right authority with confidence.

We cover six major areas: consumer rights, property & rent, employment, family & marriage, criminal matters, and RTI.
